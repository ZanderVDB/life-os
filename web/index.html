Connecting Google Calendar

Life OS can connect to Google Calendar so your real schedule appears alongside your work. This is entirely optional — you can create an account and use everything else without it, and you can disconnect at any time from Settings.

If you do connect it, Life OS asks Google for permission to:

  • See your events, so your calendar and your tasks are on one screen
  • Create, change and delete events — but only the specific change you confirm on screen first. Life OS never writes to your calendar in the background.
  • See which calendars you have, so you can choose where an event goes
  • See when you are free or busy, so it can warn you about a clash

Life OS keeps a copy of your events so the calendar loads instantly and can spot clashes. Disconnecting removes that copy. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
