My calls inside the modal:
• Birthday is read-only — pulled from any GCal event with "Jack" in a birthday-shaped title. If none found, the row says "+ Add birthday" which opens GCal's event creation with prefilled "Jack's birthday" / all-day / yearly. So GCal stays the single source of truth.
• Friendship level — open question. Two flavours: (a) manual — you just pick 1-5 yourself. Honest, no gamification. (b) XP-based — auto-levels up as you complete promises with this person (L2 at 1, L3 at 3, L4 at 7, L5 at 15). I lean (b) because you said "leveling system could be fun" and it rewards actually doing the thing.
• "Link to task" button creates a task on the Daily list with text "Jack: Go for coffee" and a small back-link badge. Ticking the task → promise moves to "Last did together" + task disappears.
• Only ONE promise + ONE last-did-together are stored per person. No history. Matches your spec.

Adding a new person
"+ Add person" button at the top of the People tab opens a small modal: name (required) + "where you met" (required-ish, prompts you). All other fields can be filled in later from the detail view. Per your call, no photos — we keep this lightweight and avoid the "fill in the form" friction.

Suggestions worth considering (your call — I'll include or skip each)

S1 · "Last seen N days ago" badge. Auto-computed from when you completed your last promise. After 90+ days, card gets a soft red tint — "you owe this person a coffee."
S2 · Tags / groups. Tag people as "uni friends", "work", "family" — quick filter chips above the grid.
S3 · AI hook. The AI bar already understands events / tasks / habits / reminders. Add an addPerson + addPromise op so you can say "remind me Jack and I said we'd grab coffee" and it lands on his card.
S4 · Birthday surfaces on Today/Daily. Already does for GCal birthdays in the morning brief — when a tracked person's birthday hits, also surface a "Wish Jack happy birthday" suggestion task.
S5 · Drag-to-reorder cards. Like the Areas tab — manual pinning lets you keep your inner circle at the top regardless of sort.
S6 · Skip the level system entirely. Cleaner, less to maintain, the "last seen" badge does the same job functionally.
My recommended cut for v1: S1 (last-seen badge), S3 (AI hook for addPerson / addPromise), S4 (birthday surface on Daily). Skip S2 (tags add complexity for a personal tool — revisit later if you have 50+ people). Skip S5 in v1 (default sort handles it). Decide manual vs XP levels.
